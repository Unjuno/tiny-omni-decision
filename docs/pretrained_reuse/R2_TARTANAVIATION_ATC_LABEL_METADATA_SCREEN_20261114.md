# TartanAviation ATC label metadata screen — 2026-11-14

## Decision

Do **not** admit this corpus as an independent audio or audio+ADS-B quality-evaluation set. Keep it as a possible future weak-supervision source only after a concrete task and group-split review. Its large machine-labeled training split does not provide enough independent human-reviewed targets to verify the project's 95% per-task quality goal.

This is a separate screen from the TartanAviation vision-video group result in [`R2_TARTANAVIATION_SOURCE_GROUP_SCREEN_20261114.md`](R2_TARTANAVIATION_SOURCE_GROUP_SCREEN_20261114.md). It does not reverse or generalize that result.

## Metadata-only inspection

All 184 pinned label Parquet shards (25,246,122 bytes total) were verified against the Hugging Face LFS SHA-256 values. The external artifact contains the per-shard hashes and row counts; the compact copy is [`R2_TARTANAVIATION_ATC_LABEL_METADATA_SCREEN_20261114.json`](R2_TARTANAVIATION_ATC_LABEL_METADATA_SCREEN_20261114.json).

Only `utterance_id`, `review_status`, confidence/agreement scalars, model-agreement count, and the boolean `callsign_matched` column were read. Transcript text and `callsign_tail` were not read. No audio or video payload, features, model, or evaluation set was opened. The utterance source's audio-bearing Parquet files were not fetched.

Observed counts:

- 531,050 rows and 531,050 unique utterance IDs; zero duplicate IDs.
- 40,899 parent-clip groups, 670 airport/date groups, and two airports in the pinned utterance IDs.
- Label statuses: 530,724 `auto`, 157 `verified`, 35 `edited`, 132 `rejected`, and 2 `non_speech`.
- Only 192 rows (191 parent clips) are human verified or edited. Even counting rejected human-reviewed rows, only 324 rows across 320 clips received a review status. The card describes approximately 326 clips reviewed; the two non-speech rows account for the difference in status totals.
- The provided corpus has only a `train` split. The source schema does not provide speaker IDs, so speaker-disjoint evaluation is not currently verifiable.

The R0 draft gate requires at least 1,000 independent examples and 200 independent source groups per named task. Human verified/edited targets fall far below both floors, even before grouping by speaker or preventing aircraft identity reuse. The majority labels were generated by an ASR ensemble and weighted ROVER; the card explicitly says they are not gold ground truth and that confidence is a ranking score, not a calibrated probability. Callsigns are snapped from ADS-B, which also creates a circularity risk for a task claiming that audio and ADS-B jointly determine the answer.

## Disposition and next gate

Status: **`REVIEW_NOT_ADMITTED_FOR_QUALITY`**. Do not use the human-reviewed subset to claim task quality or the auto labels as an independent audit. A weak-supervision training role could be reconsidered only after the intended decision task, clip/day/aircraft/speaker grouping, source-specific permissions, and an independent human-labeled evaluation cohort are established. No model fitting is justified by this metadata screen.

Reproducible shard hashes, source revisions, card hashes, counts, and the screening decision are in the companion JSON. No existing run, checkpoint, corpus, or sealed audit was changed or evaluated.
