# AVECL-UMons audiovisual source: preliminary screen

The [authors' paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2011.01018) describes real
audio-visual event classification and localization in two office rooms. It
reports 11 event classes, 2,662 single-label and 2,724 multi-label sequences,
about 5.24 hours in total. These are recordings, not independent rooms; the two
rooms are too few to support a strong unseen-environment generalization claim.
The event-label description alone does not prove that both modalities are
necessary for a decision or that the audio event is visible in each sequence.

The paper points to [Zenodo record 3965492](https://zenodo.org/record/3965492),
but the record could not be retrieved during this screen and its dataset
license, download scope, version and hashes have not been independently
confirmed. A third-party copy's license label for the *paper* must not be
transferred to the data. The corpus remains **REVIEW**. No media, annotations,
model training or evaluation was performed. Before admission, verify primary
data terms and participant conditions, group by actual recording, scene and
participants, and predeclare audio-only/video-only and mismatched-input
controls. Preserve separate room-level results and report the limited room
count rather than treating each short clip as an independent environment.
