# Luel IMU Egocentric 250k source screen — 2026-10-29

## Decision

`NOT_ADMITTED`. The dataset card marks the material All Rights Reserved and
states access is for authorized parties only, with no redistribution or use
without permission. The full corpus is gated; the preview's separate rights
were not established. Do not download or use the sample pending explicit
permission and terms.

## Source evidence

The [Hugging Face dataset card](https://huggingface.co/datasets/Luel-ai/imu-egocentric-250k)
describes egocentric RGB video synchronized with 30-Hz tri-axial accelerometer
and gyroscope data, plus structured annotations for environment, task,
objects, tools, body action, and ordered substeps. It reports a 250,000-hour
full corpus with 46,340 task records. The 10-clip preview is described as 30
minutes total and approximately 1 GB; the page says the full corpus requires
contacting Luel for access.

The same card's license metadata is `all-rights-reserved`; its text says
authorized parties only and prohibits use without permission. The page does
not identify a separate grant for the sample subset or an independent
participant/task split. A plausible structured reasoning task does not
overcome the missing usage rights, so the preview was not fetched.
